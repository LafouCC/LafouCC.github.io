Hi! I’m an incoming Ph.D. student at [The University of Texas at Austin](https://www.utexas.edu/). Previously, I obtained a B.S. in Computer Science from [New York University Shanghai](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/) and [New York University](https://www.nyu.edu/).

I have been fortunate to work with [Prof. Hongyi Wen](https://whongyi.github.io/) and [Prof. Shengjie Wang](https://sheng-jie-wang.github.io/) on personalization of diffusion models and machine unlearning for mitigating undesirable data influence in LLMs. 

I was also a Visiting Student Researcher at the [Stanford Computational Imaging Lab](https://www.computationalimaging.org/), advised by [Prof. Gordon Wetzstein](https://stanford.edu/~gordonwz/), where I worked on video world models. Broadly, my interests lie in **controllability and personalization in generative models**.

Outside of research, I am a classically trained flutist and enjoy badminton and diving.
